## (Il)liquid Markets

This is the first post in *(Il)liquid market* series.

Due to my background in spot FX trading, I find the problem of market liquidity
both: important and interesting. 

There is a quote in Taleb's
*Dynamic Hedging* that goes roughly like this: ''Market is a huge
theater with very narrow doors''.  This captures best the spirit of what you 
are dealing with when new information enters the market
and the market participants are adjusting their positioning. 
When things get really bad, [not even
the market for US Treasuries remains unchanged](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2020/04/treasury-market-liquidity-during-the-covid-19-crisis/).

In this series I will be exploring topics related to market microstructure
and order books in order to better understand the mechanics of how market
reacts.
